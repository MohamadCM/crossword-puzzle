# Part 2 — Crossword UI

For the second part of the assessment, we would like you to build a web-based crossword interface.

The goal of this exercise is **not** to implement a sophisticated crossword engine. Instead, we are primarily interested in how you approach **UI design, UX, usability, and overall product quality**.

A crossword data file is included in the folder you downloaded along with this assessment. Please use the provided data to populate your application.

## Requirements

Your application should allow a user to:

- View the crossword grid and its clues.
- Select a cell and navigate between cells.
- Enter, change, and delete answers using the keyboard.
- Clearly understand which cell and which word they are currently working on.
- Easily identify the clue associated with the currently selected word.
- Complete the crossword.
- Receive appropriate feedback as they interact with and complete the puzzle.

These are the minimum functional requirements. **How you choose to implement and present them is entirely up to you.**

## AI Usage

Unlike Part 1, **you are free to use AI tools during this part of the assessment**.

You may use AI for boilerplate code, setup, debugging, or other parts of the development process. However, we are primarily evaluating **your own UI/UX decisions, product thinking, and ability to turn a set of requirements into a polished and usable experience**.

## What We Are Looking For

We are particularly interested in:

- **UI clarity and visual quality** — Is the interface clean, coherent, and visually polished?
- **UX** — Are interactions intuitive and predictable? Does the interface guide the user naturally through the puzzle?
- **Usability** — Can someone unfamiliar with the application understand how to use it without additional instructions?
- **Attention to detail** — Have you considered states, feedback, keyboard interaction, responsiveness, accessibility, and other details that contribute to a good experience?
- **Engineering quality** — Is the implementation well structured, readable, and maintainable?

Feel free to go beyond the minimum requirements and introduce any features or design decisions that you believe improve the experience. We are interested in seeing **how you prioritize and where you choose to spend your time**.

There is no single correct solution. We will evaluate the quality of the experience you build and the reasoning behind your decisions.

## Time Limit

It should take **8 hours** to complete this part of the assessment.

Please prioritize accordingly. We do not expect you to implement every feature you can think of; a smaller, polished experience is preferable to a larger but unfinished one.

## Submission

Please submit your completed project in a compressed folder through `forge.apollo.st`.

Include a `README` containing a brief explanation of:

- The main design and UX decisions you made.
- Anything you chose to prioritize and why.
- Anything you would improve or implement differently if you had more time.
